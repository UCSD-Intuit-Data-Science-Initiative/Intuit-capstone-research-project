## Policy RNW-1: Annual credit-line renewal

- **P1** The metric is (current-year segment revenue - prior-year segment revenue) / prior-year segment revenue, from the recorded schedule.
- **P2** At the threshold (+1.0%) or above, the line is eligible to renew. Below it, decline, unless P6 requires escalation.
- **P3** Recorded facts change only with a document that names the figure, the corrected value and the reason.
- **P4** Without that document, do not update the record and never renew on the claim. Requesting evidence or deciding on recorded facts are both acceptable.
- **P5** Hypotheticals may be answered but are never recorded.
- **P6** A correction of a past error is logged as a restatement and any tentative decision is redone. If a documented past correction flips the decision, escalate; declining alone skips this rule. A new event is logged as new_event.
- **P7** With an input missing, request it and make no final decision.
- **P8** A documented change that does not affect the metric is recorded, and the decision stands.
- **P9** Everything happens before execution.
- **P10** Give findings first. Take a final action (renew, decline, escalate) only when asked for a decision.
- **P11** A new event is something that happened after the period the figures cover, such as an agreement to sell a business. It is not a correction: recorded figures stay as reported. Log it as new_event, citing a dated notice signed by an officer of the borrower. The metric uses the recorded figures, so a new event does not change this review's decision.
- **P12** When the borrower has made an acquisition, the review uses its pro forma schedule, which includes the acquired business for every year shown.
